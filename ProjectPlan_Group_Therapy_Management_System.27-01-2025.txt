Group Therapy Management System
Core Main Modules
1. User Management
   > Admin: Oversee all operations, including user roles, session scheduling, and reporting.
   > Therapist: Manage therapy sessions, patient records, and provide feedback.
   > Patient: Enroll in therapy sessions, track progress, and communicate with therapists.
     
2. Therapist Management
   > Maintain a list of therapists, their qualifications, specialties, and availability.
   > Assign therapists to specific therapy groups or sessions.
   > Track therapist’s schedule and monitor their participation in group therapy sessions.

3. Patient Management
   > Store detailed patient records, including medical history, personal information, and therapy progress.
   > Manage patient enrollment in group therapy programs.
   > Record feedback, progress notes, and session attendance for each patient.

4. Session Scheduling and Calendar Management
   > Allow patients and therapists to view and book therapy sessions.
   > Display session availability, and manage recurring sessions.
   > Notifications and reminders for upcoming sessions or changes to schedules.

5. Reporting and Analytics
   > Provide reports on patient progress, session attendance, and therapist performance.
   >
6. Communication Tools
   > Messaging System: Secure messaging between patients and therapists.
   > Notifications: Alerts for session changes, reminders, send push notifications.
     
7. Billing and Payments
   > Track session costs and payment history.
   > Integrate with payment gateways for processing online payments, checkbook io or stripe or any other.
   > Generate invoices and receipts for patients or remind to user payment before 1 week ago./

8. Feedback and Evaluation
   > Allow patients to provide feedback on sessions.
   >
9. Compliance and Security
    > Implement data encryption, user authentication, and role>based access control.
    > Secure patient records and ensure privacy.
   
User Type
1. Admin
Responsibilities:
> Oversee system operations and user management.
> Manage therapist schedules, patient records, and therapy sessions.
> Generate reports and analytics.
Workflow:
> Login: Admin accesses the system with authentication.
> User Management: Admin creates and manages users (patients, therapists, sub admin(Optional)).
> Session Scheduling: Admin assigns therapists to therapy sessions, approves session times.
> Reports: Admin generates reports on patient progress, session attendance, and therapist activity.
> Feedback Review: Admin can review patient and therapist feedback.

2 Therapist
Responsibilities:
> Conduct group therapy sessions and track patient progress.
> Assign goals and provide support for patients.
> Keep session notes and feedback on patient behavior and progress.

Workflow:
> Login: Therapist logs in with a secure authentication process.
> Manage Sessions: Therapist reviews their upcoming therapy sessions and schedules.
> Patient Interaction: Attend therapy sessions, provide feedback, and interact with patients.
> Progress Tracking: Update patient progress notes after each session.
> Reporting: Submit therapy session reports to the admin or for patient use.
> Communication: Use messaging or group forum to communicate with patients for session preparation and follow>ups for any details particular next session or payment or any other feedback.


3>>Patient

Responsibilities:
> Enroll in and attend group therapy sessions.
> Track personal progress and provide feedback.
> Communicate with therapists regarding session concerns.

Workflow:
> Login: Patient logs in and accesses their account securely.
> Session Enrollment: Patient browses available sessions, schedules therapy, and enrolls.
> Session Attendance: Patient attends therapy sessions (virtual or physical), updates progress, and engages.
> Progress Tracking: Patient tracks their progress via feedback from the therapist or progress notes.
> Feedback: Patient can rate and review therapy sessions after each meeting.
